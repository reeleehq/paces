paces.segmentation#

What a segmenter returns: Segmentation, its SegStep records and the Span they cover — the analysis-result types, with nothing else.

They live apart from paces.segmenters (the registry and the segment() facade) so a module that only consumes or reshapes a result — paces.corrections, paces.projection, paces.ask, the evidence layer — depends on the types and not on the facade, and the facade can import those modules without a cycle. paces.segmenters re-exports every name here, so from paces.segmenters import Segmentation keeps working.

paces.segmentation.inconsistencies(seg, *, max_overlap_flags=20)[source]#

What no segmentation may contain, as inconsistent: flags — never repaired, never raised: empty spans, spans of different steps that overlap, children outside their parent, and step edges missing from boundaries. paces.segmenters.segment() runs it on every result, whichever segmenter produced it and whether or not a correction log ran.

>>> inconsistencies(Segmentation(
...     steps=(SegStep(id="a", spans=((0.0, 10.0),)),
...            SegStep(id="b", spans=((8.0, 20.0),))),
...     boundaries=(0.0, 10.0, 20.0)))
("inconsistent: steps 'a' and 'b' overlap (8–10)", 'inconsistent: step edges not in boundaries: 8')
