paces.render_card#

The card renderer: one routine on a printable card, as HTML or PDF.

render_card(doc) returns a self-contained, print-styled HTML page: the title, what you need, the structure with counts, and a row per step with its poster, description, cues and cautions. It fits one or two sheets, A4 or Letter (@page carries the paper size), and uses plain tables and no script so even an old print engine lays it out. Posters are embedded as data URIs when media_from is given, so the card is one file.

write_card_pdf turns that page into a PDF through the fleet’s converter, pdfdol, an optional extra (paces[pdf]): the model and every renderer stay pydantic-only (ADR-0004), and importing this module imports nothing heavy. Without the extra, asking for a PDF says exactly what to install.

The card reads the document and one strings table; it never depends on the analyser. “What you need” is the document’s resources and “Notes” its notes; a cue’s label is printed beside it.

Convert a card page to a PDF at output through pdfdol.

engine=None is pdfdol’s default (wkhtmltopdf); engine="chromium" prints with headless Chromium (needs pdfdol[chromium]). Raises RuntimeError naming exactly what to install when something is missing.
